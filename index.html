SPLICE: Standards, Protocols, and Learning Infrastructure for Computing Education

SPLICE supports the CS education community with shared documentation, infrastructure, standards, protocols, and tools that make learning content and educational data easier to build, reuse, and study.

Access the SPLICE Portal Explore the demo gallery Meet the working groups

Reusable Learning Content

Promoting the development and broader reuse of innovative learning content instrumented for rich data collection.

Data and Analytics

Developing formats and tools for the analysis of learner data.

Research Infrastructure

Sharing best practices for making learner datasets and analytics available to CSE, data science, and learning science researchers.
