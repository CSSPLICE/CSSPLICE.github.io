13th SPLICE Workshop at SIGCSE TS 2027

Wednesday, February 17, 2027, 1:00–5:00 p.m. Pacific time (conference schedule)
SIGCSE TS conference venue, Sacramento, California

How to register

To attend, add the SPLICE Workshop when you register for SIGCSE TS 2027.

The workshop is a free half-day Affiliated Event and takes place the day before the regular SIGCSE paper sessions begin.

Who should attend?

  • Instructors who want to use SPLICE’s free catalog of learning content and authoring tools, including Parsons problems, visualizations, and worked examples.
  • Researchers who want to find collaborators, datasets, and opportunities to run studies.
  • Tool builders who want to reach more users, integrate their tools with shared protocols, and navigate common administrative challenges.

What will happen?

  • Hands-on tutorials. Learn to use SPLICE teaching and research tools.
  • Lightning talks. Share your research and find collaborators. A call for short papers is coming soon.
  • Discussions. Talk through challenges in your work, share best practices, and help shape the future of SPLICE.

About SPLICE

SPLICE provides documentation and infrastructure to help the CS education community adopt shared standards, protocols, and tools. The project promotes:

  • Developing and reusing innovative learning content that can collect rich learner data.
  • Creating formats and tools for analyzing learner data.
  • Sharing best practices for making large collections of learner data and analytics available to researchers in computing education, data science, and learning science.
